## Codex

- Use subagents as needed when a task has independent parts, such as a research question with several separate
  threads.
- Do not end a turn with a plan, an offer or a next step you are authorized to take; take it. Stop only for a decision
  the user must make, a real blocker, or an action on the ask-first list, and while a question is pending, continue
  the work that does not depend on it.
